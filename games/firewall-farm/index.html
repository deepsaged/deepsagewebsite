About this game

Grandma left you the old farm in Byte Hollow, and with it the village's entire network. Your fields grow data, your barn is a server rack, and every season brings new pests: phishing moths that mimic friendly letters, ransomware crows that lock up the silo, and password weeds that spread wherever someone reused "harvest123".

Plant strong fences, rotate your keys like crops, teach villagers to check who really sent that letter, and decide what the farm actually needs to keep. It is a slow, cozy game with real security thinking underneath: least privilege, backups, patching and privacy by design, all learned one harvest at a time.

Features

  • Four seasons of threats modeled on real attack patterns
  • Villagers with their own security habits you can help improve
  • Backups, patches and access rules that work like farm upkeep
  • A privacy almanac that decides which data the farm should never keep
