About this game

Deep in a mountain forge lives Brom, an ancient golem who will build anything you ask for, exactly as you ask for it. Ask for "a sword" and you get a sword the size of a house. Ask him to "fix the bridge" and he may fix it permanently in place. Your job as the forge's new apprentice is to learn how to ask.

Each commission is a puzzle in clear instructions: give context, set constraints, show examples, and break big jobs into steps. Villagers bring stranger requests as the seasons pass, and Brom's quirks mirror the real habits of modern AI models, so the puzzles practise the same prompting patterns you can use with today's AI tools.

Planned features

  • 60+ commissions solved by writing instructions, not by clicking
  • Brom reacts to vague wording, missing context and conflicting goals
  • Unlock rune techniques modeled on real prompting patterns
  • A sandbox forge to experiment with your own requests
