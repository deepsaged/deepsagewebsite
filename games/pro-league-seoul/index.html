Pro League Seoul

An esports career sim where you climb from PC bang to world finals, with your team talking Korean.

About this game

Start as an unknown player in a Seoul PC bang and rise to the world championship. Train with your team, review matches with the coach, give interviews and handle fans, all in Korean, from casual banter to formal press conferences.

In-game shotcalling teaches quick commands; the gaming house teaches daily life, honorifics with seniors and the slang of Korean gamer culture.

Features

  • A career from PC bang to world finals
  • Shotcalls and team talk in fast Korean
  • Honorifics with coaches, seniors and fans
  • Press conferences that train formal speech
