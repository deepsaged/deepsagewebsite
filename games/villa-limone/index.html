A cozy life sim on the Amalfi Coast where you grow lemons, make limoncello and learn Italian.

About this game

Inherit a crumbling villa with a lemon terrace on the Amalfi Coast and bring it back to life. Grow lemons and tomatoes, make limoncello and pasta, open a guest house and make friends with neighbours who speak warm, expressive Italian.

Seasons bring new words: Easter and spring markets, summer saints' festivals with fireworks over the sea, the autumn harvest and the Christmas presepe.

Features

  • Villagers with routines, friendships and southern expressions
  • Festivals of saints, harvests and Christmas
  • Italian recipes that teach kitchen vocabulary
  • A daily diary that reviews your words
