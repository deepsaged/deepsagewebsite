About this game

Match teacups, scones, maple leaves and koalas across boards set in London, Edinburgh, New York, Toronto and Sydney. Clear levels to decorate a tea room, and collect word tiles that build your English vocabulary.

Short daily sessions pair a puzzle with five new words and a review of yesterday's.

Planned features

  • Levels across the English-speaking world
  • Word tiles with British and American audio
  • Decorate a tea room with your stars
  • Daily five-word sessions with spaced review
