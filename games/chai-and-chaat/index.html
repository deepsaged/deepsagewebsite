About this game

Take over your grandmother's tiny chai and chaat stall in Old Delhi and grow it into a beloved café. Brew masala chai, make samosas, jalebi and golgappe, and serve regulars who order quickly and joke in natural Hindi.

As your Hindi grows, regulars share their stories: a rickshaw driver, a Bollywood hopeful, a poet who writes in Urdu and Hindi.

Planned features

  • Forty Indian street-food recipes with cooking mini-games
  • Regulars with story arcs in graded Hindi
  • Polite and casual Hindi practised at the counter
  • Decorate the café with lamps, rugs and plants
