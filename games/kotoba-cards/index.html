About this game

A cursed hanafuda deck has opened a gate to the spirit world. Your cards are Japanese words, nouns strike, verbs act, particles chain them, and you play them as sentences against kappa, tengu and the fox queen who guards the inner shrine.

Particles are your combo system: wa, ga, o, ni and de decide which card hits what. After each run, the words and particles you misused become a review hand.

Planned features

  • Over 400 Japanese word cards in a hanafuda-inspired style
  • Combos built on particles and word order
  • Bosses from yokai folklore
  • Kana and kanji levels you can switch at any time
