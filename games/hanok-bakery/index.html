About this game

Turn an old hanok house in Seoul's Bukchon village into the cutest bakery café in the city. Bake bungeoppang, castella and strawberry cakes, brew cafe lattes and yuja tea, and serve regulars who order and chat in natural Korean.

As your Korean grows, regulars share their stories: an idol trainee, a webtoon artist, a grandmother who knows every secret of the street.

Planned features

  • Thirty Korean café recipes with baking mini-games
  • Regulars with story arcs in graded Korean
  • Polite and casual speech practised at the counter
  • Decorate the hanok with plants, lamps and dishes
