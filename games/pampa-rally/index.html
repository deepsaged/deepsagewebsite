About this game

Drive the toughest stages of South America: Patagonian gravel, Andean switchbacks, Bolivian salt flats and the Atacama desert. Your co-driver reads pace notes in rapid Spanish, and turning left at the right moment depends on understanding "curva cerrada a la derecha tras la cresta".

Between stages, talk to mechanics, sponsors and locals to repair your car and plan the next route, learning the Spanish of Argentina, Chile and Bolivia along the way.

Planned features

  • Rally stages in Patagonia, the Andes, Uyuni and the Atacama
  • Pace notes read in Spanish at real rally speed
  • Regional accents and words from four countries
  • Service-park conversations that unlock repairs and upgrades
