A cozy countryside life sim where you grow rice, run an onsen and learn Japanese with your neighbours.

About this game

Move to a sleepy village in the mountains of Japan and restore your grandparents' old farmhouse. Grow rice and vegetables, raise chickens, open a small hot-spring inn and make friends with neighbours who speak warm, simple Japanese.

Each season brings new words: cherry blossom viewing in spring, fireworks and festivals in summer, the moon and maple leaves in autumn, the New Year in winter.

Features

  • Villagers with routines, friendships and local dialect
  • Seasonal festivals: hanami, Tanabata, Obon and New Year
  • Japanese home recipes that teach kitchen vocabulary
  • A daily diary in kana and kanji that reviews your words
