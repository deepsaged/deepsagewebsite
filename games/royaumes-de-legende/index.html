About this game

Pick a hero from French legend, Joan of Arc, a Gaulish druid, the Beast of Gévaudan or a knight of the Round Table, and battle across lanes through Brocéliande forest to an enemy château. Abilities are French verbs, and the team chat is French from your first match.

Upgrades require conjugating the verb behind each ability, so you practise passé composé and futur every time you power up.

Planned features

  • Heroes from Arthurian, Gaulish and French folklore
  • Ability upgrades earned by conjugating French verbs
  • French quick-chat that teaches real gaming phrases
  • Maps inspired by Brocéliande, Carcassonne and Mont Saint-Michel
