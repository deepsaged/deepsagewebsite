About this game

Neuron Garden turns machine learning into gardening. Plant layers of luminous neurons, connect them with vines, and feed your garden examples to learn from: pictures of birds, handwritten letters, the sounds of rain. Watch the connections brighten as it learns, and watch weeds of overfitting creep in when you water it too much with the same data.

Each greenhouse is a new problem, from sorting fruit to predicting the weather. Prune, rebalance and test your garden on examples it has never seen. It is calm, beautiful and surprisingly honest about how real models learn, fail and get better.

Planned features

  • A living network you grow layer by layer, vine by vine
  • Training, validation and overfitting shown as a changing garden
  • Greenhouse challenges covering vision, sound and prediction
  • A zen mode with no goals, only growing
