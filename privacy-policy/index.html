2. What we process, why, and on which legal basis

2.1 Visiting the website (hosting, delivery, security)

When you open a page, your browser sends technical data (IP address, date and time, requested page, referrer, browser and operating system). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare's network, which process this data to deliver the pages and protect them against abuse. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure, working website). These logs are kept by the providers for a short period under their own retention rules.

2.2 Account, wish list and sign-in

If you create an account (email and password, or Google sign-in), we process your email address, display name, a user ID, the time you signed up, your language preference and the items on your wish list. Sign-in is provided by Google Firebase Authentication; account data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and, for the wish list, in a Cloudflare D1 database. If you sign in to the DeepSage Launcher, a sign-in token is handed to the launcher on your own computer. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the account you asked for).

2.3 Game updates by email

We only send game updates and the weekly digest if you ask for them: with the separate, optional box at sign-up (it is not pre-ticked and signing up doesn't depend on it) or on your settings page. We then send you one email asking you to confirm your address (double opt-in). Until you click the confirmation link, you get nothing else from us; an unconfirmed request is not used. After you confirm, you receive a welcome email, the weekly digest and occasional game updates.

Your email address and subscription status are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database; emails are sent via Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). Brevo adds a small image to each email and routes links through its own server to count opens and clicks. We have set Brevo so that these counts are anonymous and not linked to you, and we don't use them to build profiles. This applies to every email we send through Brevo, including contact form confirmations; if you don't want it, set your email program not to load images. Legal basis for these counts: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in knowing that our emails are delivered). Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. To be able to prove your consent (Art. 7(1) GDPR), we record when you asked to subscribe and where (sign-up or settings), when you confirmed, and the IP address and browser identifier used to confirm. Legal basis for this record: Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR. We keep it as long as you are subscribed and for up to three years after you unsubscribe, the period in which claims about the emails could be raised.

You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email, on your settings page, or by writing to us; this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier emails.

2.4 Beta testing and connected accounts

If you apply to test a game in the DeepSage Launcher, we store your application, your email address and a hardware summary of your PC (processor, graphics card, memory, operating system) so we can choose a mix of machines. While you play a beta build, it sends performance data, crash reports, game progress events, results of the hardware test, and the feedback you choose to send (text, and a screenshot or voice recording only if you add one). This beta data is stored under a pseudonym that is different for each game, not under your email address or account ID. If you choose to connect a social media account (for example to share a quiz result), we store the access tokens that platform issues, only to post what you ask us to post. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can disconnect an account at any time in your settings.

2.5 Analytics, attribution and A/B tests (only with your consent)

Only if you allow "Analytics" in the cookie banner do we load Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited). It sets cookies and records page views, approximate location, device and browser information and interactions, so we can understand how the site is used. With the same consent we remember how you first found us (UTM campaign parameters, landing page, the website you came from) and, if you then create an account, store the campaign parameters, landing page and sign-up page in your account record, so we can tell which channels bring sign-ups. We do not store your IP address, browser user agent or the full address of the page you came from in your account. Only the name of the website you came from (for example "bsky.app") is counted, together with the campaign parameters, in an anonymous daily sign-up statistic that contains no account ID, IP address or exact time and cannot be linked to you. A/B tests remember which variant you saw in your browser's local storage. Without consent none of this is loaded or stored. Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. You can withdraw consent at any time via "Cookie preferences" in the footer; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

2.6 Short links

When you open a deepsage.com/s/… short link, we count the click (link code, campaign parameters, a random click ID and the time) on our Cloudflare server, without storing your IP address and without cookies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in knowing which posts lead people to the site).

2.7 Protection against abuse

To stop automated spam on the contact form, our Cloudflare server counts how many messages arrive from one IP address within a minute. This count exists only briefly in Cloudflare's memory; the IP address is not written to any database. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a working contact channel).

2.8 Contacting us

You can write to us by email or with the contact form. With the form we process the type of request you choose, your email address, your name if you give it, your message and the time it arrived; we do not store your IP address or browser identifier with it. The message is stored in our Cloudflare D1 database and forwarded to our mailbox at IONOS via Brevo, and you receive a confirmation email with a reference number (it does not repeat your message). If you email us, we process your message and contact details in our mailbox at IONOS. We use this data only to answer you and, for requests about your personal data or legal notices, to document that and when we received and answered them. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or account, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR for requests under the GDPR, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering enquiries). You don't need an account, and a request is valid whether it arrives by form or by email.

2.9 Our social media channels

We publish on social media platforms. If you comment on or reply to our posts there, we collect from the platform your public user name, display name, profile link and the text of your comment, together with our reply, so we can read and answer comments. This data comes from the platform, not from you directly (Art. 14 GDPR). It is stored on our own computer and kept for 12 months, then deleted. The platforms process your data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in running our channels and answering comments). You can object at any time (section 6).

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and wish list: until you delete your account (settings page) or ask us to delete it.
  • Email subscription: until you unsubscribe or delete your account. An unconfirmed subscription request is deleted after 30 days. After a confirmed subscription ends, we keep only the record of your consent (when and how you confirmed) for 3 years, to be able to prove it, then delete it.
  • Your ratings of our emails: 2 years.
  • Beta testing: applications, telemetry and hardware reports 12 months; feedback and crash reports with attachments 24 months; all of it earlier if you delete your account.
  • Record of a deletion request: 3 years, to show that we handled it.
  • Attribution data in your account (campaign parameters, landing and sign-up page): as long as the account exists.
  • Contact form messages: feedback and general messages until the matter is closed, at most 2 years; requests about your personal data and legal notices 2 years after they arrive, as the record that we handled them. Emails to and from our mailbox: at most 2 years, unless they are business correspondence we must keep longer (6 or 10 years, § 257 HGB, § 147 AO).
  • Rate-limit counter for the contact form: about one minute, in memory only.
  • Comments on our social media channels: 12 months.
  • Google Analytics: according to the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months).
  • Consent choice and A/B variant in your browser: up to 12 months, or until you clear your browser storage.
  • Data we must keep for legal reasons (for example tax records) is kept for the statutory period.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data (Art. 15) and receive a copy;
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • Erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • Data portability (Art. 20);
  • Object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21);
  • Withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We then stop the processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves legal claims.

While the settings page is switched off, account holders can ask us by email or the contact form for a copy of their data (access and portability) or for deletion of their account; we handle these requests within one month. When the settings page returns, "Download my data" and "Delete my account" will let you do this yourself.

For anything else, or if you have no account, email contact@deepsage.com or use the contact form. We answer within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR).

7. Minimum age

Accounts and email updates are for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Are you required to provide data?

No. You can read the site without an account. An email address is needed only to create an account or receive updates. We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects for you (Art. 22 GDPR).

9. AI-generated content

Articles, quizzes, images, videos, game and book descriptions on DeepSage are generated with artificial intelligence. Generating this content does not involve your personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our processing changes. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
