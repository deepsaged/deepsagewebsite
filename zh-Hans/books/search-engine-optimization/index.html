The Librarian of Everything

How search engines decide what you find, and how to be found

开发中

简介

Imagine a librarian who has read every page on the internet and must recommend the best one in half a second. This book explains how that librarian works: crawling, indexing, ranking and understanding what people really mean. Then it shows how to create pages the librarian loves, by being genuinely useful rather than tricking the system.

你将学到的核心概念

  • Crawling and indexing
  • Search intent
  • Content quality and relevance
  • Links and authority
  • Technical SEO basics
