The Detective in Your Head

Solving everyday mysteries with evidence, logic and a little doubt

개발 중

개요

Every day you are handed claims dressed as facts: headlines, ads, arguments, rumors. This book turns you into a detective of ideas. Through short cases, from a suspicious miracle diet to a village convinced a ghost stole the bread, you learn to question sources, weigh evidence, spot fallacies and change your mind when the facts demand it.

배우게 될 핵심 개념

  • Claims vs. evidence
  • Common logical fallacies
  • Evaluating sources
  • Cognitive biases
  • Updating beliefs
