Overview

Leadership is not a title; it is a set of choices, often small and often invisible. This book follows a reluctant new team lead through her first year, from the first awkward one-on-one to the crisis that tests everyone's trust. It covers vision, delegation, feedback and accountability through a story you will recognize from your own workplace.

Key concepts covered

  • Setting direction
  • Delegation and ownership
  • Giving and receiving feedback
  • Building trust
  • Leading through crisis
