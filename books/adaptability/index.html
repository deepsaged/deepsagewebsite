Water Finds a Way

Thriving when the plan, the job and the world keep changing

In development

Overview

A river never argues with a rock; it goes around, under or through. This book gathers stories of people who faced sudden change, from a chef whose restaurant closed overnight to a pilot improvising a landing, and draws out the habits that let them adapt instead of break. You will learn to loosen your grip on the old plan, learn fast and treat change as information.

Key concepts you will learn

  • Growth mindset
  • Learning agility
  • Reframing setbacks
  • Experimenting in uncertainty
  • Resilience habits
