Overview

Léo enters a virtual seventeenth-century Paris and becomes a Card Musketeer: every sword strike is a French word card. He must build a deck, master combos and win duels against the Cardinal's guards, while readers level up with deck lists, combo tables and grammar bosses.

Key concepts covered

  • Themed vocabulary decks
  • Regular and irregular verbs
  • Agreement and word order
  • Reflexive verbs
  • The imperative
