The Shanghai File

A 1930s noir mystery that teaches Chinese in the Paris of the East

Noir MysteryBilingual Reader
In development

Overview

In 1936 Shanghai, a detective investigates the disappearance of a jazz singer from a famous club on the Bund. Witness statements, newspapers and coded notes are in Chinese, with clue boxes that explain the grammar.

Key concepts you will learn

  • Describing people and places
  • Past and completed actions
  • Question words
  • Reading signs and newspapers
  • Linking words
