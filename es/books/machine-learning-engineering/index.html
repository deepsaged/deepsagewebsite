From Notebook to Nightshift

The journey of one model from a laptop experiment to a system millions depend on

En desarrollo

Resumen

A promising model works beautifully in a notebook on Friday and falls over in production on Monday. This book tells the story of that model's first year in the real world: messy data pipelines, drifting inputs, silent failures at 3 a.m. and the engineers who learn to catch them. Along the way you learn the craft that turns a clever experiment into a dependable service.

Conceptos clave que aprenderás

  • Data pipelines and feature stores
  • Train, validation and test splits
  • Model deployment and serving
  • Monitoring and data drift
  • Reproducibility and versioning
