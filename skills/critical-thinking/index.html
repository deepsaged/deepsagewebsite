What is Critical Thinking?

Critical thinking is analyzing claims and evidence, spotting logical fallacies and bias, and reaching well-reasoned conclusions.

Why Critical Thinking matters

Critical Thinking is one of the skills shaping how people work, learn and solve problems today. As the pace of change speeds up, it is no longer optional: it is a foundation for staying capable and confident, whether you are a student, a professional or simply curious about where the world is heading.

How to learn Critical Thinking online: the benefits of edutainment

Traditional courses and dense textbooks can make Critical Thinking feel abstract. Online edutainment, learning wrapped in game-like, interactive experiences, offers another way in:

  • Learn by doing. Interactive scenarios and challenges turn abstract concepts into hands-on practice.
  • Stay engaged. Progress, feedback and story are there to make practice something you look forward to.
  • Learn at your own pace. Short, replayable sessions you can fit around a busy schedule.
  • Build intuition. Practise decisions and see their consequences, a hands-on complement to lectures and reading.
  • Review over time. Our games are designed around spaced review, a technique that learning research links to better long-term retention than re-reading.
