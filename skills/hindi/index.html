Why learn Hindi?

Learn Hindi online: the Devanagari script, vocabulary and conversation for travel, Bollywood and business in India.

Hindi is spoken by more than 600 million people, making it one of the three most spoken languages in the world. It is the most widely spoken language of India, the world's most populous country and one of its fastest-growing economies, and the language of Bollywood cinema. The Devanagari script is phonetic: once you learn the letters, you can read words exactly as they sound.

How to learn Hindi online: the benefits of edutainment

Traditional courses and dense textbooks can make Hindi feel abstract. Online edutainment, learning wrapped in game-like, interactive experiences, offers another way in:

  • Learn by doing. Interactive scenarios and challenges turn abstract concepts into hands-on practice.
  • Stay engaged. Progress, feedback and story are there to make practice something you look forward to.
  • Learn at your own pace. Short, replayable sessions you can fit around a busy schedule.
  • Build intuition. Practise decisions and see their consequences, a hands-on complement to lectures and reading.
  • Review over time. Our games are designed around spaced review, a technique that learning research links to better long-term retention than re-reading.
