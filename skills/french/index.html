Why learn French?

Learn French online: master everyday vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation for travel, study and work.

French is spoken by more than 300 million people on five continents and is an official language of the United Nations, the European Union and the Olympic Games. It is a key language of diplomacy, fashion, cuisine and the arts, and one of the fastest-growing languages in the world thanks to Africa. Learning French also makes other Romance languages like Spanish and Italian much easier to pick up.

How to learn French online: the benefits of edutainment

Traditional courses and dense textbooks can make French feel abstract. Online edutainment, learning wrapped in game-like, interactive experiences, offers another way in:

  • Learn by doing. Interactive scenarios and challenges turn abstract concepts into hands-on practice.
  • Stay engaged. Progress, feedback and story are there to make practice something you look forward to.
  • Learn at your own pace. Short, replayable sessions you can fit around a busy schedule.
  • Build intuition. Practise decisions and see their consequences, a hands-on complement to lectures and reading.
  • Review over time. Our games are designed around spaced review, a technique that learning research links to better long-term retention than re-reading.
