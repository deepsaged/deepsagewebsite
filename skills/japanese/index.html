Why learn Japanese?

Learn Japanese online: hiragana, katakana, kanji, vocabulary and grammar for anime, travel and work in Japan.

Japanese is the language of one of the world's largest economies and of the anime, manga, video games and culture that millions of learners love. It is now the fourth most studied language worldwide. Its three writing systems, hiragana, katakana and kanji, look daunting at first, but short daily practice turns them into a puzzle you can solve.

How to learn Japanese online: the benefits of edutainment

Traditional courses and dense textbooks can make Japanese feel abstract. Online edutainment, learning wrapped in game-like, interactive experiences, offers another way in:

  • Learn by doing. Interactive scenarios and challenges turn abstract concepts into hands-on practice.
  • Stay engaged. Progress, feedback and story are there to make practice something you look forward to.
  • Learn at your own pace. Short, replayable sessions you can fit around a busy schedule.
  • Build intuition. Practise decisions and see their consequences, a hands-on complement to lectures and reading.
  • Review over time. Our games are designed around spaced review, a technique that learning research links to better long-term retention than re-reading.
