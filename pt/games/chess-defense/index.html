Sobre este jogo

Chess Defense turns the oldest strategy game into a siege. Enemy pieces march down the board in waves, each moving by its own chess rules, and you place and move your defenders to hold the back row for as long as you can.

Every wave asks the same questions a good chess player asks: which threat matters most, which trade is worth it, and which move sets up the next three. The browser prototype is being rebuilt into a full game with campaigns, new piece types and a daily challenge board.

Recursos

  • Wave-based defense where every enemy follows real chess movement
  • Campaign boards with new terrain and piece types
  • A daily challenge board shared by every player
  • Replays that show where your defense first began to crack
