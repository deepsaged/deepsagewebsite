The World

The World of DeepSage

An Edutainment Game, First and Foremost

DeepSage is an upcoming game that wraps real learning in a story worth showing up for. Every fight in the game is fought with real questions across real subjects, not button-mashing. A right answer holds the line; a wrong one costs you. The story is the wrapper. The learning is the point.

The Setting

DeepSage takes place in a science-fiction world, built to give its learning-driven battles a story worth showing up for. The details are best discovered by playing - this page stays deliberately short on plot.

What to Expect

  • Knowledge as the only real weapon. Every duel, trial, and boss fight is decided by what you actually know.
  • A wide emotional range. The tone leans on humor as its constant thread, but reaches for wonder and real sorrow too - not just spectacle.
  • A world with texture. Factions, politics, and history exist beneath the surface for players who want to dig - none of it required to enjoy a fight.
  • Characters you'll want to know more about. The people you play and meet are written to matter, not just to deliver quiz questions.

That's the shape of it - deliberately without spoilers. The rest is worth discovering when the game launches.
