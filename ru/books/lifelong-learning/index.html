The Endless Library Card

How to keep learning for the rest of your life, and enjoy it

В разработке

Обзор

School ends; learning shouldn't. This inspiring book shares the science of how we learn and the stories of people who picked up new skills at 30, 60 and even 90. You will discover techniques like spaced repetition and deliberate practice, and learn how to stay curious, build habits and make learning a joyful part of everyday life.

Ключевые понятия, которые вы изучите

  • How memory works
  • Spaced repetition
  • Deliberate practice
  • Curiosity and motivation
  • Building learning habits
