What Is DeepSage?

DeepSage is an upcoming edutainment game where knowledge is your superpower. Every battle is a quiz duel: you and your rival trade real questions on science, history, logic and more. Answer well and you win; answer wrong and it costs you. Getting better at the game means getting better at learning.

Alongside the main game we are building a catalog of educational games, each designed around a real skill, and writing one story-driven book for every skill. They will install through the free DeepSage Launcher for Windows and Linux.

Until launch, you can learn every day with free articles and quizzes that explain the tech, AI and science stories that matter.
