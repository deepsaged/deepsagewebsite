DeepSage: Learn Through Adventure

What Is DeepSage?

DeepSage is an upcoming edutainment game built around a simple idea: real knowledge should feel like a superpower. Every battle in the game is fought with questions and answers instead of button-mashing. Face a rival, and you're both trading real questions across subjects like science, history, and logic. Answer well and you prevail; answer wrong and it costs you. Skill at the game is skill at learning.

DeepSage is set in a science-fiction world built to give those battles a story worth showing up for - the details are best discovered by playing.

It's a world worth exploring - and every fight in it is a chance to actually get smarter.
